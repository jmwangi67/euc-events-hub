An initially observed sticky-date-header problem was corrected for the planner, keeping dates attached to their groups. No remaining page overflow, detached headers, or misaligned week rows were observed. Overlapping week cards retain complete clock labels by wrapping at the range boundary; long titles/metadata can still require internal vertical scrolling within their time slots, with full accessible labels and details available. The sparse synthetic month necessarily contains empty weeks, and compact full-page content is long; these are known presentation tradeoffs, not attendance/coverage claims.
